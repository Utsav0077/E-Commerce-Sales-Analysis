SQL Sales Analysis

Objective

Use PostgreSQL to calculate business KPIs and analyze retail sales performance.

Key SQL Techniques

- SELECT
- WHERE
- GROUP BY
- ORDER BY
- SUM()
- COUNT()
- COUNT(DISTINCT)
- ROUND()
- EXTRACT()
- DATE_TRUNC()
- HAVING
- LIMIT

Main Business Questions

1. What is the total sales revenue?
2. What is the total profit?
3. What is the overall profit margin?
4. How many unique orders were placed?
5. Which categories generate the most revenue?
6. Which regions are the most profitable?
7. Which products generate the highest revenue?
8. Who are the highest-value customers?
9. How do sales change month by month?
10. Which subcategories generate losses?

Example SQL Query

SELECT
    ROUND(SUM(sales), 2) AS total_revenue,
    ROUND(SUM(profit), 2) AS total_profit,
    COUNT(DISTINCT order_id) AS total_orders,
    ROUND(
        100.0 * SUM(profit) /
        NULLIF(SUM(sales), 0),
        2
    ) AS profit_margin_percentage
FROM public.superstore_sales;
